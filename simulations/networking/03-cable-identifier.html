Cable Types:

Straight-through: Same standard on both ends (T568B-T568B). Used: PC to Switch, Router to Switch

Crossover: Different standards (T568A-T568B). Used: PC to PC, Switch to Switch

Rollover/Console: Pin 1 to Pin 8, reversed. Used: PC to Router/Switch console port
