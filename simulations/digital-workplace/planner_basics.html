Assigned to
Click a name to assign or unassign. In the real thing they get a Teams notification, and the task appears in their own My Tasks list.
Due date
Quick picks stand in for the calendar picker. Overdue tasks show their date in red on the board.
Bucket
Buckets are just columns the team names for itself; moving a task tells everyone where the job is up to.
