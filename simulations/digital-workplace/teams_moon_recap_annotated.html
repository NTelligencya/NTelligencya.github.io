🎯 What to point out live

①Recording consent. The badge on the call thumbnail and the consent notice in the meeting chat only appear because attendees were told recording was starting, out loud, before it began. This is the same expectation covered in the warm-up prompt: hosts explain up front that a meeting is being recorded and why.
②The summary needs checking, not trusting. Four of the five follow-up tasks are accurate. One says six delegates are being booked on flights, when the whole project has been about four staff, the Katherine Four. Ask the room: would you have caught that before forwarding the minutes?
This is the habit to land: read Copilot's output against what you actually know before you send it on. A wrong headcount in a training scenario is harmless; a wrong headcount, date, or dollar figure in a real set of minutes is not.
