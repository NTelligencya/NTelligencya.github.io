MS Excel

// interactive skill builders

Wall of Rows → Answers: Sort & Filter

A 21-slide click-to-build tutorial for absolute beginners, no formulas anywhere: click once in the data, sort largest to smallest, and read the answer off the top row. Covers A-to-Z and largest-to-smallest sorts, two-level sorts with the Sort dialog, checkbox and number filters, stacking and clearing filters — and the classic traps, from scrambled rows to the filter nobody knew was on. Built on NT Cyber Pulse survey data.

Your First Formulas: SUM, AVERAGE & the Fill Handle

A 22-slide click-to-build tutorial for absolute beginners writing their very first formulas: the equals sign, cell references, then SUM and AVERAGE assembling one piece at a time while the matching cells light up. Covers the fill handle, how copies re-aim themselves, and the dollar-sign pin for shared cells like a GST rate. Built on a council fleet fuel log and a community pool gate tally.

Dates Are Numbers: Dates & Formatting

An 18-slide click-to-build tutorial on the secret life of Excel dates: every date is really a day number counting from 1 January 1900. Strip the formatting off and watch the number appear, subtract dates to count days, see why text dates sort scrambled, and learn the typing habits and the year-month-day format that never lies. Built on a council works request log and a leave planner.

VLOOKUP → XLOOKUP

A 30-slide click-to-build tutorial that takes a VLOOKUP user to XLOOKUP fluency: formulas assemble one argument at a time while the matching spreadsheet cells light up. Covers look-left, spilled arrays, match and search modes, and two-way lookups — then hands you three challenges to teach it yourself.

Detail → Summary: Group & Outline

A 24-slide click-to-build tutorial that folds a big worksheet into its story: group rows, collapse them with the +/− buttons and level controls, and let the Subtotal dialog build the whole outline in one hit.
