    The Prompt Critic's Checklist
    • Give the who, what, when and where. Copilot only knows what you tell it; which unit, which assessment, which date.
    • Say what you want. Source material, format, length, difficulty, and what the reader should do next.
    • Name the audience and how they read. Plain English and short sentences are a choice you make, not a fluke.
    • Supply the facts. Copilot fills every gap with something plausible; it will not say "I don't know".
    • Never send your worst mood. Ask for the tone you want to be known for; the email carries your name, not Copilot's.
    • Student work stays out of prompts. Submissions, marks and assessment content are on the never-paste list, and a prompt is a disclosure.
    • Copilot drafts, you decide. Proofread every draft; the last check is always human.
    • Keep personal and health details out of prompts. De-identify first; the message works without the name.
