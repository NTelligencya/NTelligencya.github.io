Charles Darwin University TAFE · Course 2

Microsoft Digital Skills & AI for the Workplace

A two-day hands-on course building practical digital workplace skills for Northern Territory professionals, with a focus on Microsoft 365 tools and AI-assisted workflows. Contextualised to the tools, policies, and environments you actually work in.

2 days · Weekday delivery Beginner–Intermediate Up to 15 participants Designed for NT-based organisations

Welcome

This course is built for people doing real work in NT-based organisations, government staff, community-controlled organisations, health and social services, educators, and program teams, who want to get measurably more out of Microsoft 365 and AI tools.

Over two days we'll move from the core M365 apps most workplaces already pay for, into AI-assisted workflows that save genuine time on the tasks you actually do: emails, meetings, reports, documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.

Contextualised to your environment

Examples, datasets, and scenarios are drawn from NT workplaces, regional program delivery, multi-site teams, and the realities of working with mixed connectivity. What we cover should look like the work on your desk next week.

What You'll Learn

Microsoft 365, the core toolkit. Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Outlook, OneNote, Planner and Forms, used the way they're meant to work together.

Word, Excel and PowerPoint with Copilot. How to drive the document tools you already use with AI, including formulas, drafting, formatting, and design.

AI foundations for work. What AI tools actually are, what they're good at, how to prompt them well, and how to judge the output.

AI-assisted workflows. Practical patterns for email triage, meeting notes, first drafts, summarisation, and research, with attention to what stays inside your tenant and what doesn't.

Responsible use. Privacy, information security, policy alignment, and the guardrails that matter for government and community-sector work.

Who It's For

NT Government staff, Aboriginal community-controlled organisation workers, health and social-service staff, educators, program staff, and admin teams who use Microsoft 365 in their day-to-day role.

No deep IT background is assumed. If you log into a work computer, use Outlook and Teams, and want your tools to do more of the heavy lifting, this course is built for you.
