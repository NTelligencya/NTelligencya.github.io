Resources

An annotated library of AI resources for the Northern Territory and beyond: things to watch and listen to, papers and articles worth your time, websites and tools you can try today, and the legislation and guidelines shaping how AI is governed in Australia. Every entry carries a short annotation explaining what it is and why it earns its place; nothing is listed that hasn't been vetted.
