Articles & Papers

The reading list behind the training material on this site: foundational research papers, peer-reviewed studies, benchmark analyses and threat intelligence. Some papers are academic publications that may require institutional access; where available, open-access versions have been linked.

Jump to: Foundational Papers · Research & Studies · Benchmarks & Analysis · Data Sources & Training · Content Authenticity · Threat Intelligence

Foundational Papers

NeurIPS 2017 · Vaswani et al.

Attention Is All You Need

The foundational paper introducing the Transformer architecture that underlies modern LLMs including GPT, Claude, and Gemini. Essential reading for understanding how AI language models work.

OpenAI, NeurIPS 2020 · Brown et al.

Language Models Are Few-Shot Learners

The GPT-3 paper demonstrating that large language models can perform tasks with minimal examples. Introduced the concept of "in-context learning" that defines modern AI interactions.

Baack et al. · 2025

Towards Best Practices for Open Datasets for LLM Training

Outlines the role of open, well-governed datasets in building transparent and trustworthy AI systems. Essential reading for understanding training data governance. No vetted public link yet; the title is searchable.

Research & Studies

International Journal for Educational Integrity · December 2023

Weber-Wulff et al. AI Detection Study

Peer-reviewed international study examining fourteen AI detection systems. Concluded none achieved reliable accuracy across tasks, with paraphrased and hybrid text particularly problematic. No vetted public link yet; the title is searchable.

Research dataset · Date unavailable

WildChat Visualiser

Interactive tool for exploring how people actually use AI chatbots in the wild. Valuable for understanding real-world AI usage patterns in educational contexts.

Benchmarks & Analysis

Arena · Ongoing

Arena Leaderboard (formerly LMArena / Chatbot Arena)

Crowdsourced AI benchmark using blind comparisons: users vote on which response is better, creating Elo-style ratings that reflect real-world preference rather than synthetic benchmarks. Rebranded from LMArena to Arena in January 2026.

Artificial Analysis · Ongoing

Artificial Analysis

Independent benchmarking platform comparing AI model performance, pricing, and speed. Useful for making informed decisions about which AI tool to use for specific tasks.

Data Sources & Training

Common Crawl Foundation · Operating since 2008

Common Crawl

Non-profit organisation providing free web crawl data used in LLM training. Understanding Common Crawl helps explain both the capabilities and limitations of AI systems.

BigCode Project · Date unavailable

The Stack Dataset

Large collection of permissively licensed source code used for training code generation models. Important for understanding how AI coding assistants are developed.

Content Authenticity

Adobe-led coalition · Ongoing

Content Authenticity Initiative

Coalition led by Adobe establishing open standards for content provenance. Addresses deepfake concerns through cryptographic verification of media origin and editing history.

Industry coalition · Ongoing

C2PA (Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity)

Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity developing technical standards for certifying digital content origin. Essential for understanding future content verification approaches.

Threat Intelligence

Anthropic Research · Ongoing

Anthropic Threat Intelligence Reports

Regular reports documenting real-world AI misuse patterns including the August 2025 report on agentic AI weaponisation, North Korean employment fraud, and ransomware development.
