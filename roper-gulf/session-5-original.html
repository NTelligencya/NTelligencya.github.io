Wrap Up, Questions, and AI Image Generation

A relaxed close. We pull the course together, leave plenty of room for questions, and run one memorable activity: the whole group generates the same picture, and no two come out the same. It is the clearest way to show what generative AI really does.

Session 5 running order

A light recap of the whole course

A walk back through the five sessions: staying safe, writing with Copilot, working in Teams, and the Excel basics.

0:15

The activity: everyone generates the same picture

At the same time, everyone asks Copilot for a picture using the exact same words. Then compare screens around the room. No two pictures are the same, and that is the whole point.

Everyone types

Show me a picture of a crocodile driving a Hilux.

0:30

Why is everyone's different?

Because generative AI does not look anything up. It learned what things look like from millions of labelled pictures, then paints a fresh one from random speckle every time, guided by your words. Different starting speckle means a different crocodile for everyone. The companion explainer and a real labelled dataset show what learning from examples means.

How Image Generation WorksOpens in a new window CIFAR-10 labelled image setCat, dog, bird, car, truck · new window

With enough labelled examples, a machine can learn to tell a crocodile from an alligator, the snout, the teeth, the colour, even though nobody wrote those rules down. Nobody has labelled that set yet, which is exactly why it is a good thing to imagine building.
